# Hints/tips for ROD Interactive Game

- Make note of how much of each resources is lost for different options and remember the tradeoffs when having to pick again.

- The game is balanced in that there is one 'right' path to take in every stage, but you can make up for a 'wrong' choice by using resources wisely.

- You lose 10 hunger at the and of each stage no matter what, so make sure to eat if you are around 20 or below hunger.

- Morale is the hardest status to gain back but it is the hardest to lose - it may be a wise option to keep it high.

- You can click the buttons for activities in the transition stage multiple times and they will have more effects (NOTE: multiple clicks will not be visibly displayed, feature not implemented).

- The 'right' choice is not always the 'canon' or story-accurate choice, but rather the one that would make the most sense in a survival situation (in the developer's opinion).

- To get the good ending, you need to have at least 60 research by the end of stage 7 so plan carefully!

# Recommended successful path:

STAGE 1: help him pack
STAGE 2: walk beside river
STAGE 3: leave peace offering
STAGE 4: look for Simplicio
    - in transition: eat
STAGE 5: use medicine
STAGE 6: slow down and conduct research
    - in transition: eat * 3
